01 About

About me

I like the unglamorous half of the stack — the crawler that pulls more than it did yesterday, the ingestion job that can't drop a record, the query that shaved itself in half, the endpoint that went from five seconds to under one. Systems people never think about because they simply never break.

I'm a data platform engineer with a BSc in Software Engineering from the Addis Ababa Institute of Technology. My career sits between data platforms at scale and full-stack product work: Node.js and Express at the core, TypeScript everywhere, React on the front, Python and Django when the job calls for it. I'm a Data Platform Engineer at Bazaarvoice, and since June 2026 I've also been leading the Computer-Simulated Case project for USMLE Step 3 at Synapse. Based in Addis Ababa, working remote with teams in the US and Singapore.
